Host

Mike Frascogna III

Episodes hosted: 1

Mike Frascogna III is a graduate of MC Law and practices at Frascogna Law Group alongside his father, Mike Frascogna Jr., and his brother. He grew up around a firm whose clients ranged from gospel quartets to professional sports, which taught him early that every lawyer has a story worth hearing.

He first sat across from Adam Kilgore as a guest on Adam’s MPB podcast, pinch-hitting for his father, and spent the next year plotting to turn the tables. Episode 001 is where he finally did.

    Adam Kilgore

    Longtime General Counsel, The Mississippi Bar

    Adam Kilgore served more than two decades as general counsel to the Mississippi Bar, and he never planned on law. He wanted to be a sportscaster, worked in sales, and took the LSAT after a Myers-Briggs talk with a psychology professor. He clerked at the Mississippi Supreme Court and says being genuine wins interviews.
