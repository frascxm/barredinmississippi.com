Privacy

What happens here
stays here.

Barred in Mississippi is a podcast. We built the site so your visit stays yours.

What we collect

Nothing about you. This site has no accounts, sign-ups, forms, comments or checkout.

Cookies and tracking

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers. Fonts, images and video are served from this site.

Episode players

When you press play on an episode, the video loads from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube’s own privacy policy applies from that moment.

Server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs (such as IP address, browser and time) to deliver and protect the site. We do not use them to identify visitors.

Not legal advice

Barred in Mississippi is for conversation only and is not legal advice. Guests speak for themselves.
