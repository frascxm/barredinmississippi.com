About the show

Every lawyer has a story.

Barred in Mississippi is a podcast about the people who practice law in this state. Not the cases on the evening news, but the careers behind them: why someone chose the law, how they got through the bar, and what the work is really like once the door has their name on it.

Each episode, one Mississippi attorney sits down in our speakeasy studio for an unhurried conversation. Closing attorneys and trial lawyers, general counsels and solo practitioners, people just sworn in and people who have seen it all. They tell the stories lawyers usually save for each other.

The show is independent. It doesn’t market a firm or sell legal services, and nothing said on it is legal advice. It’s conversation, the kind that used to happen after hours, with the good bottle open.
